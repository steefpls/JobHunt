Job-site logins (in the JobHunt browser)

Opens a browser window. Log in yourself (Singpass, 2-step codes and captchas are fine), then close the window. JobHunt keeps that session for hunts. It never sees or stores these passwords.

There is deliberately no Google button: the browser that fills in applications should not be signed in to your email. Jobs that need "Sign in with Google" are handed to you as Needs you.

Saved logins for application sites (optional)

Some jobs send you to a company application site (Workday, Greenhouse...) that needs an account. If you save a login here, the apply step can sign in: it asks the app to type the password and never sees it. A password is only ever typed into a password box, and only on the sites you list (the list is required). Without a saved login, those jobs are marked Needs you with the link.
